Brand exploration · Round 1

DiamondFinish — logo concepts

Three directions for a Florida epoxy flooring company, each with a palette sampled from the logo that carries straight into the website. Every mark is vector, holds up at favicon size, and works on light and dark backgrounds.

01

Mirror Gloss

Modern · premium · clean

A cut diamond whose lower half dissolves into gloss lines, like its reflection in a freshly coated floor. That mirror shine is exactly what customers are paying for. The geometric wordmark and cool sapphire-and-ice palette feel precise and high-end without being flashy. On the site, the reflection comes to life as a real glossy floor that the hero stands on.

02

Florida Terrazzo

Local · warm · one of a kind

A diamond cut from a chip floor, the speckled finish that goes into garages, lanais and pool decks all over the state, in colors borrowed from old-Florida terrazzo. Every version of the logo is a small sample of the product. The soft serif and the palm, coral and sunshine palette feel warm and local, and nothing like the black-and-chrome epoxy crowd.

03

Black Diamond

Bold · luxury · showroom

The D of DiamondFinish, cut like a gemstone, with a diamond-shaped window at its heart. Champagne-gold facets on black catch the light the way a metallic epoxy floor does. Wide, confident capitals feel like a luxury car badge, which suits the people who want a showroom garage. The premium, high-ticket option of the three.
